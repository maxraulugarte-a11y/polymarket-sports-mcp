# Conditional edges by wallet segment (2026-11-12)

Generated by the copy pipeline from each wallet's settled, pre-game, held-to-settlement bets (entry 5-95c, stake ≥ $10). ROI = flat-stake copy at the trader's own price, no fees. **lcb90** = one-sided 90% lower bound; **shrunk** = empirical-Bayes ROI (prior fitted across all buckets), so small samples are pulled toward the average. 891 segments analysed (keep/watch plus every segment with ≥ 50 clean bets), 7061 buckets tested, so a few false positives are expected. Treat in-sample results as hypotheses, not proof.

## Key findings (2026-11-12)
- **Best coin-flip pickers we already copy:** chongchong37 NBA spreads (manual trial; 66.7% wins at ~50c over 72 bets, lcb90 +13.8%), BreakTheBank NFL spreads (58.7% at 49c, n=150), vibing123 CS2 map handicaps (61.3% at 52c, n=120), 0xb4F978… CFB spreads (62.2% at 52c, n=135), ic4cream CFB spreads (57.4% at 51c, n=242), and Gooooooollllllllll LoL kill totals (keep).
- **Strong 40-60c pickers we do not copy:** lightotunnel35 CFB spreads (78.9% at 52c, but only 57 bets), 0x6e82b93e… and NM-P on **NBA totals** (n≈300 each, +8 to +11 pt edge), Venusian and MadridTime on NBA spreads (~+11 pt, n≈100), and HomeRunHazard's CFB/NFL totals. Totals are excluded from tiers by design (the walk-forward test found them noisy). These are the main candidates if you want to relax that rule, probably as a trial like ianf2026/chongchong37.
- **Conditional specialists** (overall near flat, one bucket strong in both halves of history): mostly **60-85c favourites** in esports (vibing123 CS2 moneylines, n=185, +12.7%; forsenxd Dota; Gooooooollllllllll CS2). Under the coin-flip preference these rank lower. Size-based edges are rare: hi774c MLB moneylines at 2-3x his usual size (+26%, n=56) and CAcap CBB at >3x (n=47) are the only ones that pass, and both are thin.
- **The 40c floor:** pooled across copied segments, sub-40c bets were not worse (+14% flat ROI vs +7% for 40-85c), but that pool is biased toward winners and longshot ROI is very noisy. By trader: **royalestake gains nothing below 40c** (+1.2% on 127 bets vs +10.9% at 40-60c), so the floor is right for him. **imjustkenisghey CFB moneylines below 40c** are the one clear exception (+57% on 92 bets, lcb90 +26%), so a per-segment exception there is worth considering.
- **High win rates on 70c+ favourites are mostly low value:** 75 segments win ≥ 70% at 70c+ with no clear ROI (for example ztao NBA moneylines, 81.6% wins at 80c, +1.9% ROI).
- **Caveat:** these figures are in-sample, and with about 7,000 buckets tested a few false positives are expected. Shrinkage, the 90% lower bound and the both-halves check reduce this but don't remove it.
